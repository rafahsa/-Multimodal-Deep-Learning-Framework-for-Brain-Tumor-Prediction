## PART C — Revised Section 3 (Paste-Ready)

### 3 Experiments

#### 3.1 Data and Evaluation Protocol

We use BraTS 2018 with 285 subjects (210 HGG, 75 LGG) and four MRI modalities (T1, T1ce, T2, FLAIR). We employ three evaluation settings:

(1) **5-fold stratified cross-validation (patient-level):** Each patient appears in exactly one validation fold. All metrics in Section 3.5 reported as mean ± std are computed over the five per-fold validation sets.

(2) **Full out-of-fold (OOF) predictions (n=285):** OOF predictions are aggregated across folds. ROC, precision–recall, and calibration curves (Figures 1–3) are computed on this set to avoid leakage.

(3) **Held-out threshold selection set (n=86, seed=42):** OOF predictions are split 70/30 (seed=42); 30% (86 subjects) is held out for threshold selection. Confusion matrices (Figure 4) and operating thresholds are evaluated on this held-out set only.

#### 3.2 Implementation Details

Models are implemented in PyTorch. ResNet50-3D, SwinUNETR-3D, and DualStreamMIL-3D were trained per fold. The meta-learner is a logistic regression trained on OOF probabilities. Post-hoc Platt calibration was fit on 70% of OOF predictions; operating thresholds were selected on the held-out 30%. [TODO: add GPU and runtime from logs]

#### 3.3 Metrics

We report AUC, accuracy, precision, recall, F1, specificity, and false positive (FP) and false negative (FN) counts. Calibration is evaluated with Brier score and expected calibration error (ECE). All metrics are reported as mean ± standard deviation across five folds, except for Figures 1–3 (full OOF) and Figure 4 (held-out n=86).

#### 3.4 Baselines and Compared Models

Base models: **ResNet50-3D** (3D CNN), **SwinUNETR-3D** (3D Swin transformer), and **DualStreamMIL-3D** (MIL with dual-stream attention). OOF MIL predictions were obtained during validation with **sequential** slice selection; MIL training uses **ROI** sampling. The final inference pipeline uses **entropy-based top-k slice selection (k=16)** for the MIL branch (`run_final_ensemble_inference.py`). The ensemble is a logistic regression meta-learner on base-model probabilities. SwinUNETR-3D is the best single model and is compared directly with the ensemble.

#### 3.5 Results Summary

**Table 1** reports AUC and F1 (mean ± std) across 5-fold CV for base models and the ensemble.

On the full OOF set (n=285), SwinUNETR-3D and the ensemble achieved AUC 0.9065 and 0.9126, respectively (Figure 1). **Figure 1** shows ROC curves, **Figure 2** precision–recall curves, and **Figure 3** calibration curves on full OOF predictions. **Figure 4** shows confusion matrices on the held-out threshold selection set (n=86) for the calibrated ensemble: panel (A) balanced threshold τ=0.41 (TN=19, FP=4, FN=4, TP=59; Precision=Recall=F1=0.9365) and panel (B) high-sensitivity threshold τ=0.38 (TN=17, FP=6, FN=3, TP=60; Recall=0.9524).
